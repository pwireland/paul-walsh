02 — ABOUT

Curiosity has been the constant.

I am a computer scientist who has spent more than 30 years moving between artificial intelligence research, entrepreneurship, teaching, product development and applied innovation. I currently lead AI innovation at The Dock, Accenture's global innovation centre. I also serve on the Board of Directors of the Technology Industry Alliance.

Earlier chapters include a B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Science (1989), a Ph.D. in machine learning, four years as a Senior Research Fellow at the University of Edinburgh, teaching as a Professor of Computer Science, founding a life-sciences technology company, and developing artificial intelligence systems across a wide range of industries.

My current interests include artificial intelligence, innovation, AI-enabled products and operating models, AI Software Engineering, AI in the software development lifecycle, and agentic AI workflow automation.

Outside technology I read, write, run and create.
